Privacy Policy

Reage Aí · last updated: November 2026

Reage Aí is an app for recording reaction videos on iPhone. This policy explains, in plain terms, what the app does with your data. In short: everything stays on your iPhone. The app does not collect, send or sell any of your data.

What the app accesses and why

  • Camera: to record your reaction.
  • Microphone: to record your voice during the reaction.
  • Photos and videos you choose: you pick the video or photo you react to with the iPhone's own picker. The app only receives the item you chose and has no access to the rest of your library.
  • Saving to Photos: the app only adds the videos you export to your Photos library. It does not read or delete anything in your library.

You can change these permissions at any time in iPhone Settings › Reage Aí.

Where your videos are kept

Recordings, copies of the media you imported and each project's settings are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. The Cutout effect and all image and sound processing happen on the device. Nothing is sent to servers.

You can delete recordings at any time in the Recordings list. Deleting the app removes everything from the device. Temporary export files are deleted automatically.

Images of your face and body (face data)

Reage Aí does not collect face data. The app does not perform facial recognition, does not identify people, does not create face maps, measurements or geometry, and does not store any biometric data.

The Cutout effect uses Apple's own person segmentation feature (Vision framework), which runs entirely on your iPhone. For each camera frame, it computes a temporary mask with the person's outline, used only to compose the image in real time. The mask stays in memory only and is discarded right after each frame. It is not saved, never leaves the device and is not used for any other purpose.

No image of your face or body is sent to servers, to artificial intelligence services or to any third party, including Apple. The app does not use any online AI service.

The only thing stored is the video you record yourself, inside the app on your iPhone, until you delete it (see "Where your videos are kept").

What the app does not do

  • It has no sign-up or login.
  • It uses no third-party analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting tools.
  • It does not send your videos, photos, voice or image to the internet.
  • It does not sell or share data with anyone.

Subscription

The Premium subscription is purchased and managed by Apple through your App Store account. Reage Aí does not receive or store your card or account details. The app only checks with Apple whether your subscription is active. Learn more in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Sharing

When you share an exported video (for example, on Instagram or TikTok), you do it yourself through the iPhone share menu, and the destination app's rules apply.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including minors.

Your rights

Since the app does not collect personal data, we hold no data about you to access, correct or delete. The data on your iPhone stays under your control. If you have any questions, contact us.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever handles data differently, this page will be updated first, with the new date at the top.
